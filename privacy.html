Privacy Policy – JaoBong Studio

Last updated: 11 October 2026

JaoBong Studio ("the App") is a private publishing tool operated by the owner of the "เจ้าบ๊อง" (@jaobongcat) Facebook Page and TikTok account. It is used only by the page owner to upload short animated videos that the owner created to the owner's own Facebook Page and TikTok account. The App is not offered to the public.

1. Information we access

  • Facebook: the list of Pages the owner manages, the Page ID, Page name and a Page access token, used only to publish Reels to the owner's own Page.
  • TikTok: the owner's basic profile information (display name, open ID) and an access token, used only to upload videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

The App does not access, collect or process personal data of any other Facebook or TikTok users, followers, commenters or viewers.

2. How the information is used

Access tokens are used only to upload and publish videos and captions chosen by the owner, and to read the status or link of those posts. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared with third parties, or uploaded to any other server.

4. Data retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the owner uses the App. The owner can delete all stored data at any time by removing the App's local configuration file, and can revoke the App's access at any time in Facebook Settings → Business Integrations, or in TikTok Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. To request deletion, contact us at the email below; we will delete all related data within 30 days.

5. Contact

Email: komtana.p@gmail.com
